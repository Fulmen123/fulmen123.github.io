Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information the SafePlate app collects, why, and what you can do about it. SafePlate is run by Fulmen Labs ("we"). You can reach us at safeplate-support@googlegroups.com.

The short version

  • You can use SafePlate without an account.
  • If you sign in, we keep your email address and the votes, reviews and suggestions you post.
  • Your reviews are public in the app, but they never show your email address or the name on your Apple or Google account.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We don't store your location.
  • You can delete your account, and everything linked to it, from inside the app at any time.

Using SafePlate without an account

Your location, only if you allow it

SafePlate uses your phone's location to show places near you and how far away they are. To find nearby places, the app sends your current position to our server with that request. We use it to answer the request and don't store it. You can turn location off in your phone's settings and search for an area instead.

Area search

When you search for an area, what you type goes through our server to Google Places, which finds the matching areas. If you've allowed location, your rough position goes with it, so nearby results come first. We don't keep your searches.

To keep searches to a fair number per person each day, the app makes a random ID for your phone. Our server stores only a scrambled (hashed) form of that ID and of your internet (IP) address, and deletes them within two days. They aren't linked to your account.

Kept only on your phone

Your saved places, your settings and filters, and whether you've seen the welcome screens stay on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

If you sign in

You only need an account to vote, write a review or suggest a place. You can sign in with Apple or Google.

  • Sign in with Apple gives us your email address (or a private relay address, if you choose "Hide My Email") and an ID for your Apple account.
  • Sign in with Google gives us your email address, your name, your profile picture and an ID for your Google account. We use the email address and the account ID to create your SafePlate account and recognise you when you come back. Our sign-in provider stores your name and picture with your account record, but SafePlate doesn't show or use them.

What you add while signed in is stored with your account, so that each person counts once and can change or delete what they posted:

  • Votes: one vote per menu item or safety feature.
  • Reviews: your star rating, your gluten-free safety score, any tags you pick and any text you write. Reviews are public in the app. They don't show your email address or the name on your Apple or Google account.
  • Reports: if you report a review, we record that you reported it. A review that three members report is hidden.
  • Suggestions: the place you suggest, your answer about its gluten-free options, and any note or website you add. We use suggestions to decide which places to add.

How we use information

Only to run SafePlate: to sign you in, count each person's vote once, show reviews, look at suggested places, stop abuse and keep the service working. We don't sell or rent your information, use it for advertising, or build profiles of you.

If you're in the EU or the UK, the legal basis is the service you've asked for (your account and what you post) and our legitimate interest in keeping SafePlate safe and working (search limits and technical logs).

Google user data

If you sign in with Google, SafePlate's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use your Google email address and account ID only to sign you in and to show you which account you're signed in with. We don't share them with anyone except our sign-in and database provider, Supabase, which stores them for us, and we never use them for advertising.

Companies that handle information for us

  • Supabase runs our database, sign-in and server, and stores your account and posts for us. (Supabase privacy policy)
  • Google draws the map (Google Maps) and finds areas for area search (Google Places, called from our server). Google receives the map areas you view and standard information about your device. If you sign in with Google, Google handles that sign-in. (Google privacy policy)
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple. (Apple privacy policy)
  • Restaurant photos, menus and websites load from where they are published, such as the restaurant's own website or Google. Those sites see an ordinary web request from your phone, as when you open a web page.

These companies may store or process information on servers in other countries. We don't share your information with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and posts: until you delete your account.
  • Search-limit records: two days at most.
  • Technical logs kept by our providers, such as IP addresses and request details: a short time, for security and troubleshooting.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Delete account. This deletes your account and your votes, reviews, reports and suggestions from our database straight away.

You can also email us and we'll delete your information. Removing SafePlate in your Apple or Google account settings stops that sign-in from working, but it doesn't delete your SafePlate account, so use Delete account first.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it, by emailing safeplate-support@googlegroups.com. We'll answer within one month. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

SafePlate isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has made an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Information travels between the app and our server encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules let only you change or delete your own posts in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

SafePlate

Find gluten-free food near you, and see how clearly each place marks it.

Coming soon to the App Store

What SafePlate does

  • A map and list of restaurants and cafés with gluten-free options, near you or in any area you search.
  • Each place's menu, with the dishes the restaurant marks gluten-free picked out.
  • Places that are fully gluten-free, shown clearly.
  • Votes, reviews and suggestions from other gluten-free diners. Sign in with Apple or Google to add yours.

Always check with the restaurant. SafePlate's information comes from restaurants' own menus and websites and from other diners. It can be wrong or out of date, so ask the staff before you order, especially if you have coeliac disease.
